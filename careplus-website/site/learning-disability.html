The problems we solve

What gets in the way today, and how CarePlus fixes it

The problem

Hoists, wheelchairs and specialist beds need regular servicing that's easy to miss.

With CarePlus

Equipment passports with upkeep schedules, inspections and repair history.

The problem

Sharing information with families has to respect each person's wishes.

With CarePlus

A family portal where sharing follows the person's recorded wishes, needs independent approval and can expire.

The problem

Contractors and suppliers chase jobs and orders by phone.

With CarePlus

A partner portal where they see only the open jobs and orders for their company.

The problem

Some sites have poor signal.

With CarePlus

Offline checks that sync when the connection returns.

How you'll use CarePlus

From first conversation to everyday use

  1. Set up services and people

    We configure your services, people and equipment with you.

  2. Record each person's wishes

    Decide together what can be shared, with whom and for how long.

  3. Invite families and partners

    Families use the family portal. Contractors and suppliers use the partner portal.

  4. Work on site, online or off

    Support workers complete checks wherever they are.
