For clinics, GP practices and community teams

Your review, carried out exactly.

When you change a patient's medicines, care providers on CarePlus record the change in their eMAR with a reason and a named person, pass it to the pharmacy and keep stock in step. You get fewer follow-up calls and safer care.

The problems we solve

What gets in the way today, and how CarePlus fixes it

The problem

Medication changes from a review don't always reach the people giving the medicines.

With CarePlus

Care providers record every change in their eMAR, with a reason and a named person. Records can't be overwritten.

The problem

Pharmacies dispense the old dose after a change.

With CarePlus

Providers send change notices to their nominated pharmacy, which acknowledges them.

The problem

Missed or refused doses only come up at the next appointment.

With CarePlus

Providers see missed, refused and PRN doses during the round, and trends over time.

The problem

Controlled drugs prescribed for end-of-life care need careful oversight.

With CarePlus

Providers keep a two-witness controlled drugs register and an append-only stock ledger.
