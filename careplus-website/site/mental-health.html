What gets in the way, and how CarePlus helps

These are the problems we hear most often from mental health services, and how CarePlus solves each one.

The problem

Equipment checks are tracked on separate lists and get missed.

With CarePlus

An equipment workspace with passports, scheduled checks and fault reporting.

The problem

Stock runs low or goes out of date without anyone noticing.

With CarePlus

Stock levels with expiry warnings and reorders through the supplier marketplace.

The problem

Healthcare agency bookings and compliance are hard to control.

With CarePlus

A healthcare agency workspace for bookings, workers, timesheets, compliance and spend.

The problem

Managers are buried in reports.

With CarePlus

A manager copilot gathers evidence from your records with links to the source. You decide what to do.
