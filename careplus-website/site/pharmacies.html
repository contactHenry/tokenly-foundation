The problems we solve

What gets in the way today, and how CarePlus fixes it

The problem

Reorders arrive by phone, fax and email in different formats.

With CarePlus

Electronic reorders from the provider's supply continuity tools.

The problem

Dose changes after a hospital stay or GP review get missed.

With CarePlus

Change notices from the provider's medication records, which you acknowledge.

The problem

Urgent shortages are spotted too late.

With CarePlus

Providers track stock in each home, so urgent shortages are flagged early.

The problem

Delivery questions tie up your team.

With CarePlus

Delivery tracking that both you and the provider can see.

Built for pharmacies

How you'll use CarePlus

From first conversation to everyday use

  1. A provider nominates you

    Care providers on CarePlus add you as their nominated pharmacy.

  2. Confirm the connection

    We confirm your details and how you want to receive orders.

  3. Receive and acknowledge

    Reorders and change notices come through the connection.

  4. Update delivery status

    Mark deliveries as planned and dispatched.
