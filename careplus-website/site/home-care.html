The problems we solve

What gets in the way today, and how CarePlus fixes it

The problem

Late and missed visits only come to light when a family calls the office.

With CarePlus

A live monitor with alerts and an escalation ladder, so late calls are picked up within minutes.

The problem

Rotas and runs are rebuilt by hand every week.

With CarePlus

Visit scheduling, rota building and route sequencing with travel time included.

The problem

Medicines run out in clients' homes.

With CarePlus

A household medication ledger, supply continuity checks and a connection to the client's pharmacy.

The problem

Payroll takes days of reconciling paper timesheets, travel and mileage.

With CarePlus

Approved visits flow into pay runs covering travel, mileage, sleep-ins and holiday pay.
