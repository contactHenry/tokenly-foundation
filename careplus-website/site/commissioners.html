You fund care, so you need to know it's being delivered. When providers you work with use CarePlus, you can follow delivered hours, quality evidence and statutory notifications in a read-only commissioner portal, without waiting for quarterly returns.

What gets in the way, and how CarePlus helps

These are the problems we hear most often from commissioners and local authorities, and how CarePlus solves each one.

The problem

Delivered hours are reported late and by hand.

With CarePlus

Commissioned hours compared with delivered hours, from the provider's own records.

The problem

Quality evidence differs from provider to provider.

With CarePlus

Consistent evidence packs and saved reports.

The problem

Notifications arrive through many different channels.

With CarePlus

Statutory notifications tracked, with clear status.

The problem

Monitoring visits need a lot of preparation.

With CarePlus

A read-only portal that's always up to date.
