For hospitals and NHS trusts

Discharge to care, without the loose ends.

A safe discharge depends on what happens after the patient leaves. Care homes and home care providers on CarePlus admit patients through a tracked process and check every medicine against your discharge letter. Agencies on CarePlus can also send you compliant nurses and carers.

What gets in the way, and how CarePlus helps

These are the problems we hear most often from hospitals, and how CarePlus solves each one.

The problem

Medication changes made in hospital are missed when the patient arrives at their care home.

With CarePlus

Care providers on CarePlus reconcile each discharge line by line: kept, changed, started or stopped, with a named person signing it off.

The problem

Discharges stall while a care home works through its admission paperwork.

With CarePlus

Care homes manage referrals, pre-admission assessments, rooms and admission checklists in one tracked workflow.

The problem

Home care packages start late after discharge.

With CarePlus

Home care providers schedule the first visits, set up medication records and get carers there with the app.

The problem

Wards are short of nurses and carers.

With CarePlus

Agencies on CarePlus supply staff whose DBS checks and training are tracked, and blocked when they lapse.
