What gets in the way, and how CarePlus helps

These are the problems we hear most often from nursing homes, and how CarePlus solves each one.

The problem

Paper MAR charts hide missed or unsigned doses until an audit finds them.

With CarePlus

Medication rounds run on eMAR, with missed, refused and late doses flagged while the round is still going.

The problem

Controlled drug registers and stock balances drift out of step.

With CarePlus

A controlled drugs register with two-witness sign-off and a central, append-only stock ledger.

The problem

Medication changes get lost when residents go into hospital and come back.

With CarePlus

Transitions of care reconcile every admission, transfer and discharge line by line.

The problem

Inspection preparation takes weeks of chasing evidence.

With CarePlus

Audits, a compliance framework and a medication inspection pack keep the evidence ready every day.

Services you can use today

Everything that makes those fixes possible

Each of these is already built and working for services like yours.

Medication rounds and eMAR

Unit-by-unit rounds, PRN and refusals, with a printable MAR when you need paper.

Controlled drugs

Register entries need two witnesses. Balances come from the same append-only ledger.

Stock ledger and cycle continuity

Track every medicine from delivery to administration and keep the next cycle on time.

Transitions of care

Admissions, hospital stays and discharges reconciled against the source documents.

Residents and handover

Resident profiles, care tasks and shift handover in one record.

Incidents, risks and notifications

Incident investigations, a risk register and statutory notifications with due dates.

Skills and competencies

Medication competency checks, appraisals and a skills matrix for every nurse and carer.

Audits and inspection pack

Scheduled audits and a ready-made medication pack for inspectors.

From your first conversation to everyday use

Here's how you'll join CarePlus, and where you'll use it once you're in.

  1. We set up your home

    Units, beds, residents and medicines are loaded with you during onboarding.

  2. Invite your team

    Give each person a role. They only see and do what that role allows.

  3. Run care from any device

    Nurses record rounds on a tablet or computer while managers watch oversight dashboards.
