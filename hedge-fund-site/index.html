Who we are

A partnership built around one responsibility: protecting the capital entrusted to us.

Vesperline was founded in 2009, after the financial crisis, by three portfolio managers who had seen sound positions abandoned because the capital behind them could not wait. They built a firm that sizes every investment to survive a downturn first, and judges itself over full market cycles, not quarters.

Today we are 186 people across three offices. The firm is owned entirely by its eleven partners, who invest most of their own wealth in our funds on the same terms as our clients.

How we invest

We decide how much we can afford to lose before we decide what to buy.

01

Research

Every investment starts as a written thesis, with the case for it, the case against it and a date to review it.

02

Risk sizing

An independent risk team sets the loss limit and position size before any trade is placed.

03

Review

Positions are reviewed on schedule, or as soon as they use half their loss limit. A broken thesis is closed, never averaged down.

Governance

Independent at every point where it matters.

Independent administrator

A third-party administrator calculates every net asset value.

Annual audit

Each fund is audited every year by an independent accounting firm.

Four prime brokers

Assets are spread across four prime brokers and two custodians.

Independent boards

Every fund board has a majority of independent directors.
